Healthcare EHR analysis

Cleaned and explored more than 5,000 synthetic healthcare records, then built Power BI views of patient demographics, utilization and test outcomes.

Power BIExcelPower QueryDAX

This case study follows a synthetic electronic health record dataset from preparation through reporting. The analysis focuses on how patient demographics, service utilization and test outcomes can be organized into views that are easier to review.

Approach

Records were cleaned and transformed with Power Query and Excel, then explored in Power BI. DAX measures support the report views. The dataset is synthetic and does not contain real patient information.
